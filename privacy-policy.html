Privacy Policy — Empty Mirror Uploader

Last updated: September 2026

"Empty Mirror Uploader" is a small command-line tool that automates uploading videos to, and managing comments on, the YouTube channel Empty Mirror (@EmptyMirrorZen). The channel is owned and operated by the same Google account that authorized this tool.

Data the tool accesses

  • Your YouTube channel — uploading videos, setting thumbnails, and reading video metadata.
  • Your channel's comments — reading, replying to, and moderating comments.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos you have produced to your own channel.
  • To help you review and respond to comments on your channel.

Where data is stored

All credentials (the OAuth refresh token) are stored locally on your own computer at ~/.hermes/youtube/token.json. No data is transmitted to, or stored by, any third-party service.

Data sharing

This tool does not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. It does not collect analytics, and it does not use advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account Security > Third-party apps page, or by deleting the token.json file on your computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the channel operator via @EmptyMirrorZen.
